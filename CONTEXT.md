# Prashna

Prashna is a free, sign-up-free tool for CBSE teachers in India to design question papers and their marking schemes, then print or export them. It is used as an installable web app, with a landing page that introduces it.

## Language

### The paper

**Paper**:
The one question paper a teacher is currently designing, including its details, sections and questions. A device holds one Paper at a time; other papers are kept as saved files.
_Avoid_: Document, test, exam, question set

**Paper Details**:
The header information printed at the top of a Paper: school name, examination title, class, subject, time allowed and general instructions.
_Avoid_: Metadata, paper info, header

**Section**:
A lettered part of a Paper (A to E) whose questions share a marks-per-question value and a question count.
_Avoid_: Part, group, block

**Question**:
One item in a Section, of type Standard, MCQ, Assertion–Reason or Case-Study.
_Avoid_: Item, entry

**Alternative Question**:
The "OR" question printed with a Question that students may answer instead.
_Avoid_: OR question, choice question, optional question

**Target Marks**:
The total marks the teacher intends the Paper to add up to, used to check progress.
_Avoid_: Max marks, goal, total

**Science Division**:
The Physics, Chemistry or Biology label on a Class 9–10 Science Question, used to balance a combined Science Paper.
_Avoid_: Science branch, sub-subject

**Marking Scheme**:
The answers to a Paper's Questions, with how marks are awarded, shown or printed alongside the Paper.
_Avoid_: Answer key, solutions, answer sheet

**Template**:
A ready-made Paper (sections, questions and details) that replaces the current Paper when loaded.
_Avoid_: Format, pattern, preset, sample

**AI Generated Paper**:
A complete Paper with its Marking Scheme that Prashna writes with AI from a Class, Subject, Blueprint and optional Focus, and that replaces the current Paper when loaded, as a Template does.
_Avoid_: AI paper, auto paper, sample paper, generated template

**Blueprint**:
The official CBSE pattern for one Class and Subject: its Sections, question types, marks and time allowed.
_Avoid_: Pattern, guideline, design, scheme

**Focus**:
The optional chapters or topics a teacher asks an AI Generated Paper to concentrate on; without it the Paper covers the full syllabus.
_Avoid_: Topic filter, syllabus selection

### Reusing questions

**Question Bank**:
The teacher's own collection of saved Questions on their device, which they can insert into any Paper.
_Avoid_: Library, repository, online bank

**Starter Questions**:
The ready-made Class 10 and Class 12 Question sets built into Prashna that a teacher can add to their Question Bank.
_Avoid_: Auto-fetch, online questions, curated bank

**Import from Link**:
Adding Questions to the Question Bank from a question file at a web address the teacher provides.
_Avoid_: Fetch, sync, download questions

### Getting the app

**Landing Page**:
The public page that explains Prashna and leads teachers to get the app.
_Avoid_: Homepage, website, marketing site

**Get the App**:
The single call to action that installs Prashna, or explains how to on devices without an install prompt.
_Avoid_: Download, install now

**Update Notice**:
The small "New version available" message the app shows when a new version of Prashna has been downloaded, offering Refresh to open it. The app never reloads by itself.
_Avoid_: Update prompt, upgrade banner, toast
