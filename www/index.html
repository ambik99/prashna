Everything a CBSE Paper needs

Question types, answers, printing and offline use (4 features).

  1. CBSE question types

    [Sections A–E]

    Standard, MCQ, Assertion–Reason and Case-Study Questions, each with an optional “OR” choice. The marks blueprint counts every Section against your target total, and you can type in Hindi and Bengali too.

  2. Marking Scheme

    [Answers]

    Write the answer as you add each Question. Show the Marking Scheme beside the Paper, or print it on its own for the evaluators.

  3. Print and Word export

    [A4 · PDF · Word]

    Print on A4 or save as PDF, laid out like a Board Paper. Or export to Word and make last changes in your own document.

  4. Works offline

    [No internet]

    Once it has opened, Prashna works without internet. Your Paper saves on your device as you type, so closing the app loses nothing.

How it works

From a blank Paper to a printed one (4 steps).

  1. 1

    Set up

    Enter the school, examination, class and subject, or start from a ready-made Template.

    Add questions

On your computer and your phone

On a computer, the editor sits beside the Paper. On a phone, Edit, Preview and Bank sit at the bottom of the screen, within reach of your thumb.

Get the App and Prashna opens from your home screen like any other app, full-screen and ready without internet.

Your papers never leave your device.

We only count anonymous visits.

There is no account and no sign-up. Your Papers and your Question Bank are kept in the browser on your device. To move a Paper to another device, save it as a file and load it there.

Questions teachers ask

How do I install Prashna on an iPhone?

Open this page in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen. Tap Get the App above and Prashna shows you the two steps. On an Android phone or a computer, Get the App installs it in Chrome.

Is it free?

Yes. Prashna is free, with no sign-up and nothing to pay.

Is Prashna an official CBSE app?

No. Prashna is made by a teacher and is not affiliated with CBSE. It follows the Section pattern CBSE Papers use, so you can set Papers the way you already do.
